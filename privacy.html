The short version

Everything you log stays on your phone unless you sign in. Signing in backs it up, syncs it and lets you share a Blok with people you choose. We run no analytics and no advertising, and we never sell your data.

1. Using MemoBlok without an account

MemoBlok works fully signed out. Everything you enter stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if you lose or reset the phone.

2. When you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. Once you are signed in, we store:

  • the Bloks you track, their settings, goals and goal history;
  • your entries: values, dates, notes, tags, subcategories, and a place if you chose to add one;
  • your Repeat schedules and reminder settings;
  • your own categories, units, subcategories and tags;
  • a display name, if you share a Blok;
  • whether you have Premium; and
  • an account identifier and the email address Apple or Google gives us. With Apple this may be a private relay address.

3. Sharing a Blok

When you share a Blok or join one, the entries in that Blok go to its other members, except their location, which never leaves your account. Members see your display name.

A member's phone keeps a copy until it next syncs after the sharing ends. When someone leaves, their display name stays on the group's record of who took part. Deleting the account removes it.

Any member can report another. We store both display names and the reason given, and we read reports ourselves. Text other members will see is checked on your phone against a short word list, and nothing is sent anywhere for that check.

4. Location

Adding a place to an entry is optional. MemoBlok only ever asks for “while using the app” access and never collects location in the background.

When you log with a place, the app takes one position reading. To name the place, your phone's built-in geocoder sends the coordinates to Apple. The map in the app loads its tiles from Apple Maps.

5. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. There is no push service and no reminder data on our servers beyond your settings. The one other notification is a reminder two days before a free trial ends, and only if you already allow notifications.

6. Premium purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and managed with RevenueCat. We store whether your membership is active. We never see your payment details.

7. Who processes your data

We use these providers, only on our instructions and under written agreements:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions.
  • PowerSync: backup and sync.
  • RevenueCat: purchases. It receives an app user ID and your purchase history.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, place names and maps.
  • Google: Google Sign-In.

8. Emails to support

Send Feedback and Report a Problem open an email draft that includes the app version, your phone model, the iOS version and the last few error codes. Nothing is sent until you press Send. Your phone keeps at most 20 codes, and none of them leave it on their own.

9. What we do not collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of your data for advertising. MemoBlok does not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone or health data.

10. This website

www.memoblok.com sets no cookies, runs no analytics and makes no requests to any other server. Our web host keeps standard request logs, including IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

11. Why we process your data

In the UK and EEA, our legal bases are: performance of a contract, for your account, backup, sync and sharing; consent, for adding a place to an entry, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings; and legitimate interests, for keeping the service secure and answering support email. Data may be processed in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it and everything stored with it from our systems. Backup copies are overwritten on a rolling cycle. Data kept only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

13. Your rights

You can get a copy of your data at Settings › Export, and delete your account at Settings › Delete account. You can also ask us to correct, restrict or move your data, or object to how we use it. California residents have the same rights under the CCPA, and since we never sell or share personal information there is nothing to opt out of. Write to support@memoblok.com from your account's email address, and we will reply within 30 days.

14. Children

MemoBlok is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 to create an account, or older where your country sets a higher age.

15. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest on our systems, and access controls mean an account can only read its own data and the Bloks shared with it. On your phone, iOS protects it with the app sandbox and device encryption.

16. Changes to this policy

When we change how we handle your data, we update this page and its date. Material changes are also announced in the app.
