Your data

Delete your account and data

You can delete your MemoBlok account and everything backed up with it at any time.

Fastest

In the app

  1. Open MemoBlok and go to Settings › Delete account.
  2. Sign in once more with Apple or Google to confirm it's you.
  3. Confirm. Your account and everything backed up with it are deleted right away.

No longer have the app?

Email us from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. Writing from that address is how we know the request is yours. If you can't, write anyway and we'll find another way to check.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your account and its email address
  • Every Blok, entry, goal, note, tag and place
  • Repeat schedules, reminders and your own lists
  • Your membership record
  • Your shared Bloks stop being shared, and your entries in other people's shared Bloks go with you
  • Your display name, including on other members' records of shares you were in

What stays

  • Data still on a phone you hold. Delete the app to remove it.
  • Your Apple or Google account. We only remove our record.
  • Reports made about you, with your display name, kept to deal with abuse.
  • Support email, only as long as we need it to help you, and records the law makes us keep.
  • Backup copies, for up to 7 days.

Before you delete

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Premium. Your membership lives with the App Store, so cancel it there first.

Manage subscriptions →

Want a copy first?

Settings › Raw Data File makes a free copy of everything before you go. Deletion can't be undone.

Only want to stop backing up?

Signing out stops backup and takes your Bloks off this phone. Your backup stays until you delete the account, and signing back in brings everything back.
