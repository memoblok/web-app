Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. MemoBlok is fully usable signed out, and nothing you enter leaves your device. An account is only for backup and syncing between devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes, entirely. The app never waits on a network. If you are signed in, changes sync the next time you have a connection.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and nowhere else unless you sign in. If you do, a backup copy is also stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

What happens when I sign in?

Signing in links the data already on your device to an account and turns on backup and syncing. Nothing is uploaded before you sign in, and signing out leaves your data on the device.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email ayen@memoblok.com from your account's email address and we will send you a copy within 30 days.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. To delete an account and the data backed up with it, follow the steps on Delete your data.

Is it free?

Yes. MemoBlok will be free to download and free to use. If we ever add anything paid, we will describe exactly what it costs and what it includes before you are asked to buy it.

Which platforms do you support?

iOS and Android. MemoBlok has not been released yet — it is coming soon to both. There is no web version.
