Track anything. See what it adds up to.

memoblok turns the things you do into Bloks — habits, spending, workouts, weight, reading, anything you can count or measure — and turns your entries into streaks, goals and trends you'll actually read.

Coming soon to iOS and Android

What memoblok does

One app for every kind of tracking, built so that logging takes a second and reading your history takes no effort at all.

Count, amount or measurement

Tally a habit, add up an amount, or track a value like weight. Each Blok logs the way it should, in the units that make sense for it.

Goals that fit your rhythm

Set a target per day, week, month or year, in either direction — “at least 8 glasses”, “at most $50” — and change it whenever your routine changes.

Log in a tap

Quick-add straight from your list, or open an entry to record a value, a note and a few labels to sort it by later.

Insights that stay quiet

A single plain-language read on what your data is doing — and nothing at all when there's nothing worth saying.

Trends across everything

Streaks, your best days, and how each Blok is moving over time — with everything you track in one place.

Yours, on your device

Everything works offline and lives on your phone. Sign in only if you want a backup.
