A few things to know

What is included free?

Up to 5 active Bloks with the Doodle icons, plus logging, goals, reminders, Insights, Trends and Compare. Backup and sync, joining shared Bloks and a full copy of your data are free too. Archived Bloks don’t count toward the five.

What does Premium unlock?

Unlimited Bloks, every icon style, Repeat for entries on a schedule, share links to invite people, and spreadsheet and printable report exports. Choose monthly or yearly, and yearly starts with a free week for new members. If your membership ends, you keep everything you made.

Do I need an account?

No. MemoBlok works fully without one, and everything stays on your phone. Sign in with Apple or Google when you want a backup, want MemoBlok on another device, or want to share a Blok.
