About

The company behind MemoBlok

Who we are

MemoBlok is built by MEMOBLOK, LLC, a California limited liability company, founded in 2025.

What we do

We build personal tracking software for iOS and Android. Our first product, MemoBlok, is an app for tracking anything you can count or measure — habits, spending, workouts, weight, reading — and for turning those entries into streaks, goals and trends that are actually worth looking at.

We build for people who want their own data back, so the app works offline and keeps everything on the device by default. An account is optional and exists only to back up and sync what you have already logged. We run no analytics and no advertising, and we do not sell data.

MemoBlok has not been released yet. It is coming soon to iOS and Android.
