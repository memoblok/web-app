About

The company behind MemoBlok

Who we are

MemoBlok is built by MEMOBLOK, LLC, a California limited liability company, founded in 2025.

What we do

We build personal tracking software for iPhone. Our first app, MemoBlok, tracks anything you can count, add up or measure, and turns your entries into goals, insights and trends worth reading.

It works offline and keeps everything on your phone by default. An account is optional: it backs up what you log and lets you share a Blok. We run no analytics and no advertising, and we never sell data.
