What sharing means

One Blok, nothing else

Only the Blok in the invitation is shared. Your other Bloks and the rest of your account stay yours.

Add or follow along

You'll see the entries in that Blok, and depending on the invitation you can add your own.

Places stay private

Where an entry was logged never crosses to anyone else. Joining is always free.

Not sure about this invitation? You never have to accept one, and an unused invitation expires on its own. Something looks wrong? Write to us before you accept.
