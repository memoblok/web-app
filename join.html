Invitation

You've been invited to share a Blok

memoblok turns the things you do into Bloks — habits, spending, workouts, weight, reading, anything you can count or measure. Someone would like to share one of theirs with you.

Coming soon to iOS and Android

Already installed? Tap the link again. Your phone only checks for memoblok at the moment you tap, so the second tap is the one that opens the app and your invitation with it.

What sharing means

Accepting an invitation connects exactly one Blok, and nothing else about your account changes.

It goes both ways

The person who invited you will be able to read the entries you log in that shared Blok, and you'll be able to read theirs.

Nothing else crosses

Only the Blok in the invitation is shared. Every other Blok you track stays yours alone, and so does the rest of your account.

Not sure about this invitation?

You never have to accept one, and an invitation that goes unused expires on its own. If something looks wrong, write to us before you tap.
